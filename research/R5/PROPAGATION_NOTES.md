**Cross-cutting caveat that propagates everywhere:** every LOCAL_REPRO ran on WSL2 Ubuntu 26.04, kernel 6.18, as uid 1000 with sudo group membership, on a VM with no DMI and no NVMe. The target is bare metal, Ubuntu 24.04, kernel 6.8 (7.0 pending), with real DMI, NVMe and an IPMI device. Where a fact depends on hardware or kernel version — sysfs shape (R5-F21), `os.Root` behaviour (R5-F22/F23), orphan reaping (R5-F14) — the host confirmation is filed as R5-OR2..OR5 and the design degrades explicitly rather than assuming parity.
